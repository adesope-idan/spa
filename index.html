    What services does Beauta offer?

    We offer a range of relaxing beauty and wellness treatments, including massage, facial treatments, and hair treatments. Each service is designed to provide a comfortable and refreshing experience.

    How can i make my appointment?

    You can book your preferred treatment through our online booking form or contact our team directly. We recommend booking ahead, especially for busy periods and weekends.

    How long does a treatment session takes?

    Treatment times vary depending on the service you choose. Most sessions take between 45 and 90 minutes. The exact duration will be provided when you make your booking.

    Can i request an at-home treatment?

    Yes. Selected treatments can be provided in the comfort of your home. Simply let us know when making your booking so we can confirm availability and arrangements.

    Do you offer any packages?

    Yes. We offer carefully selected packages that combine different treatments into one relaxing experience. Our packages are designed to give you more value while allowing you to enjoy multiple treatments during one visit.
